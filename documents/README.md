# Research Papers For Reasoning-Rationale Distillation

This folder contains the core papers referenced by the thesis pivot toward structured rationale distillation for low-label entity resolution.

| File | Paper | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `wadhwa-2024-learning-from-natural-language-explanations-for-generalizable-entity-matching.pdf` | Wadhwa et al. 2024, Learning from Natural Language Explanations for Generalizable Entity Matching | Closest free-text explanation distillation baseline. |
| `steiner-2024-fine-tuning-large-language-models-for-entity-matching.pdf` | Steiner, Peeters, and Bizer 2024, Fine-tuning Large Language Models for Entity Matching | Structured explanations for EM fine-tuning; useful contrast because it is not compact seq2seq distillation. |
| `peeters-2023-entity-matching-using-large-language-models.pdf` | Peeters, Steiner, and Bizer 2023/2025, Entity Matching using Large Language Models | LLM-as-matcher and structured explanation/rule background. |
| `zeakis-2026-distiller-knowledge-distillation-in-entity-resolution-with-large-language-models.pdf` | Zeakis et al. 2026, DistillER: Knowledge Distillation in Entity Resolution with Large Language Models | Closest ER knowledge-distillation paper and important novelty boundary. |

Original sources are arXiv PDFs.
